# FAQ — canned answers to common client questions

Living document; add new Q&As as they come up. When a client asks one of these,
reproduce the answer closely, personalizing lightly.

**Do you need a meet and greet?**
Meet & greet is optional with me. However if you'd like we can set one up!

**Can we do a meet and greet?**
Absolutely, we can meet at the Brownie Neighborhood Park (Google link: https://share.google/ivIxh2Hpd5QRY59iv). I'll bring Brownie so the two pups can meet each other and get used to one another's scent. What does your availability look like for the next 7 days? What time frame are you available generally?

**If they insist on doing it at my place:**
Sure! You can bring your pup over. My address is 9708 Cottle Dr, and for the introduction I would take your pup inside by myself first. Assuming everything goes well I'll bring both my pup & yours out so you get to see how they get along as well. Does that process work for you?

**My dog's not neutered/spayed, is that ok?**
Yes! As long as they are not aggressive, Brownie gets along with everyone!!

**I have a puppy and they're really young and haven't finished all their vaccines — can you board them?**
Definitely. I have a lot of experience taking care of young puppies. As far as vaccination goes, since your dog will only be in contact with my dog and my dog is up to date on all of her vaccines, I don't see any issue there.

**I would like to do a video call, can we do that?**
Sure! What time today works for you?
